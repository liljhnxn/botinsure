1. Executive Summary

In traditional Web3 insurance protocols, token holders or centralized committees vote on whether to approve insurance claims. This introduces an inherent, catastrophic agency conflict: capital underwriters vote directly on whether to pay out their own capital, leading to prolonged delays, claim denial scandals, and destroyed user trust.

BotInsure solves this structural flaw by establishing an entirely parametric protection framework on Botchain Mainnet (Chain ID 677). Policies possess pre-determined, binary trigger metrics sourced directly from trusted on-chain oracles (IBotOracle) and DeFi protocol states (IBotLend). Payouts execute deterministically without dispute windows, manual paperwork, or discretionary voting.

3. Protocol Architecture & Lifecycle

The lifecycle of a BotInsure policy follows an immutable state-machine pattern:

  1. Capital Underwriting: Liquidity providers deposit BOT tokens into dedicated risk pools and receive yield-accruing ERC-20 share tokens (BotInsurePoolToken).
  2. Policy Acquisition: Users select coverage parameters and duration. 95% of premium deposits directly into pool assets, while 5% allocates to protocol treasury.
  3. Capacity Reservation: The smart contract locks the exact coverage commitment, preventing LP capital withdrawals that would violate solvency.
  4. Condition Monitoring: On-chain oracles report real-time feeds. Decentralized keepers continuously monitor active policies.
  5. Deterministic Payout: If a trigger condition is verified, the policy becomes CLAIMABLE. The user claims their payout within 7 days with zero committee friction.

4. Financial Engineering & Underwriting Economics

Premiums are computed transparently on-chain using precision fixed-point math:

Premium = (CoverageAmount * PremiumRate * Duration) / (10,000 * 365 days)

Liquidity Pool shares follow a pro-rata Net Asset Value (NAV) model:

  • Deposit: SharesMinted = (DepositAmount * TotalShares) / PoolNetAssets
  • Withdrawal: BOTRedeemed = (SharesBurned * PoolNetAssets) / TotalShares
  • Solvency Invariant: PoolNetAssets - WithdrawalAmount ≥ LockedCoverageObligations

5. Smart Contract Security & Architecture

BotInsure is built with production-grade defense-in-depth principles:

  • Reentrancy Protection: OpenZeppelin ReentrancyGuard on all deposit, withdraw, purchase, and settlement flows.
  • Safe Token Transfers: OpenZeppelin SafeERC20 handles standard and non-standard ERC-20 behavior.
  • Oracle Staleness Guards: Smart contracts reject feeds older than maxOracleAge or non-positive values.
  • Single-Use Policy Guards: Once settled, policies transition permanently to Claimed.
  • Emergency Circuit Breakers: Role-based Pausable mechanism can pause new purchases during black-swan events while allowing valid claims to settle cleanly.
  • Comprehensive Test Coverage: 35 automated Hardhat unit tests covering pool share math, product limits, double-claims, and financial invariants.
